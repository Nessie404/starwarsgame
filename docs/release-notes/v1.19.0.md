# WiiKart 1.19.0

Weather on the original speedway, a boost meter built from scratch, a
no-guts-no-glory AI, and corners across the roster opened out so the
shoulder is no longer a shortcut.

## Weather

CLASSIC now carries three patches of road. Each one starts as snow at
the green flag, melts into ice, and melts again into a puddle — on its
own clock, staggered from the other two, so the track is never in
lockstep with itself. Tire compound actually matters for it:

- **Soft** is the tire to have in snow or ice, and the worst choice
  once the same patch has become a puddle.
- **Hard** is exactly the other way round — the puddle tire, poor in
  snow and ice.
- **Medium** never wins or loses that trade; it's the same everywhere.

The timings and every grip multiplier live in the new `weather` block
of `settings.json`. No other circuit gets any weather — see `TODO.md`
if you want to take this to the mountain passes next.

## Boost

WiiKart had an engine-trait boost system from v1.14 through v1.15 and
retired it entirely in v1.16. This is a different, simpler mechanic
built new:

- A meter charges while the engine is turning real revs, following a
  curve rather than a flat rate — time spent near the limiter counts
  for far more than the same time low in the band.
- Hitting the boost button spends whatever is charged in one shot for
  an instant speed bump. A half-full meter is half the bump, not the
  same bump sooner.
- Any gear change — up or down, automatic or manual — wipes the meter
  back to zero. Working it means holding a gear near the limiter on
  purpose.

Default bindings: F and H on the two keyboard players, GameCube/Xbox
Y, and Minus on a bare Wii Remote or Classic Controller — all editable
in `controls.json` like every other action. The AI uses it too, and
knows not to launch itself into a corner too hot to make it: it only
fires with real speed headroom ahead.

## A wilder AI, and a fairer fight

The whole AI field got a competitiveness bump, and two drivers —
TANAKA and CROSS — now race on a brand new **AI_YOLO** strategy sheet:
the most overconfident, most attacking, least defensive driving style
in the roster, riding every gear to the limiter (which, conveniently,
also charges their boost fastest). The weakest couple of drivers got a
skill bump so they're no longer plain slow, while the roster keeps a
genuine top and bottom.

## Corners opened out, shoulders closed off

Every barriered track used to leave several meters of only lightly
penalized dirt between the paved edge and the guardrail — enough room
to straight-line a corner by running wide across it. That gap is now a
tight, consistent curb everywhere. Berthoud, Loveland, Monarch and
Guanella are also scaled up to widen their tightest turns without
redrawing them by hand; Berthoud's summit switchbacks — the tightest
corners and the closest the road comes to itself anywhere on that lap
— specifically benefit.

## Also in this release

The bones of a difficulty preset (Easy/Normal/Hard) are in place —
`DifficultyPreset` and `GameConfig.difficulty` in `game.h` — describing
what a future menu choice would set for lap count, AI aggressiveness,
AI car choice and guardrails all at once. Nothing reads it yet; see
`TODO.md` for what wiring it up for real still needs.

## Playing it

Download `wiikart.zip`, unzip, and open `wiikart.dol` in
[Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org) (File > Open). On a real Wii, copy the
`apps` folder to an SD card and launch it from the Homebrew Channel.

To edit `cars.json`, `settings.json` or `controls.json` in Dolphin you need
an SD card: Config > Wii > Insert SD Card, put the `apps` folder on it, and
launch `boot.dol` from there. The JSON CONFIG screen on the main menu tells
you what was actually read.
