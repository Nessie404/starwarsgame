# WiiKart 1.21.0

An automatic turbo, two more drivers worth worrying about, and some of
the field genuinely hunting the racing line.

## Boost, rebuilt as a turbo

The old boost was a meter: charge it by holding a gear near the
limiter, spend it all at once on the use button for an instant speed
bump. It's gone.

A turbo now spools up entirely on its own while you're genuinely on
the throttle at real revs — no button, nothing to manage — and bleeds
off on its own the moment you lift or brake, the way a real turbo's
boost pressure drops without exhaust flow to drive it. Whatever is
spooled applies straight to engine power, every frame you're on the
gas. A quick gear change just holds the spool steady instead of wiping
it, so short-shifting through a band isn't punished the way it used to
be. The power bonus itself tapers by how much grip you're already
spending on cornering — full effect in a straight line, next to none
mid-slide, the way a traction control would back off boost rather than
piling more power onto tires that are already at their limit.

The "use" button does something different now: for as long as it's
held, it's exactly as if the gas pedal were on the floor and the brake
untouched — an instantaneous full-throttle stab, not a resource to
spend. Tunable in the renamed `turbo` block of `settings.json` (it was
`boost`).

## Two more drivers worth worrying about

HOLT was, for a while, the one driver a human genuinely struggled to
beat. **KESSLER** and **DUARTE** join the field, replacing RENARD and
SOLANO:

- **KESSLER** is HOLT's kind of trouble — late braking, rides the
  limiter, commits and rarely pays for it. Arguably a little more
  consistent than HOLT, if anything.
- **DUARTE** is a different problem entirely: a metronome who takes
  the tightest line on the track lap after lap and essentially never
  puts a wheel wrong.

## Some of the field hunts the real racing line

A human chasing a lap time reads well past the corner they're
actually in — the reason to run wide into one bend is often the shape
of the next one. **OSEI**, **NORDLI**, and **DUARTE** now do the same
thing, each reading a different distance ahead of the immediate apex,
so they feel like different people finding the line rather than one
setting worn by everybody. That anticipation fades on its own the
tighter the corner they're actually in gets — a driver mid-hairpin is
committed to that corner, not still weighing what comes after it.

## Playing it

Download `wiikart.zip`, unzip, and open `wiikart.dol` in
[Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org) (File > Open). On a real Wii, copy the
`apps` folder to an SD card and launch it from the Homebrew Channel.

To edit `cars.json`, `settings.json` or `controls.json` in Dolphin you need
an SD card: Config > Wii > Insert SD Card, put the `apps` folder on it, and
launch `boot.dol` from there. The JSON CONFIG screen on the main menu tells
you what was actually read.
