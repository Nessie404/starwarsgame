# WiiKart 1.20.0

Oversteer and understeer that actually mean something, a sharper AI, a
puddle that costs you more than grip, and the data-only bones of team
and career modes.

## Oversteer and understeer, rebuilt

Understeer used to cost speed at a flat rate the moment you pushed past
the grip limit. It now scrubs progressively — a corner taken barely too
hot just runs a little wide, one taken way too hot really pays for it.

Rear-driven cars also get something new: **power oversteer**, separate
from the existing handbrake drift. Commit hard to a corner on the
throttle and a rear-driven car can rotate faster than pure grip would
allow, for free, for as long as you keep asking for more than the car
can grip. Ease off in time and it settles back down having gained real
rotation over an identical front-driven car — catch it, and it rewards
you. Keep the wheel locked over past a second of that and it spins:
real speed lost, no steering input for a while, a real mistake.

Both are tunable in the new `understeer`/`oversteer` blocks of
`settings.json`.

## A sharper AI

The field's skill multiplier is up again (1.02 → 1.06), and so is how
much of its own braking distance the AI trusts itself with (0.72 →
0.76 of the theoretical maximum) — it brakes later and corners closer
to the real limit than before.

## Weather, fleshed out

Standing water now drags at every car regardless of tire choice, on
top of the existing grip trade — a puddle costs top speed as well as
cornering grip (`weather_puddle_drag_mult` in the `weather` block).

The AI can see it coming, too. Its corner-speed lookahead now discounts
grip for whatever weather patch is ahead of it on CLASSIC, slowing for
an upcoming snow, ice, or puddle segment the same way it already slows
for a corner it has learned to respect — instead of assuming dry
pavement everywhere and finding out the hard way.

## Also in this release

Two more data-only "bones," in the same spirit as v1.19.0's difficulty
preset — described, not wired up:

- **Team mode.** `TeamDef`, `team_defs[]`, `team_name()`, and
  `GameConfig.team_mode`/`team[]` describe four teams, each identified
  by a paint colour. Nothing reads `team_mode` yet.
- **Career/campaign mode.** `CareerState`, `career_record_result()`,
  and `GameConfig.career[]` describe a human's grid slot defaulting to
  where they finished the last race instead of always starting at the
  back. Nothing persists it to disk, and `game_init` doesn't read it
  yet.

## Playing it

Download `wiikart.zip`, unzip, and open `wiikart.dol` in
[Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org) (File > Open). On a real Wii, copy the
`apps` folder to an SD card and launch it from the Homebrew Channel.

To edit `cars.json`, `settings.json` or `controls.json` in Dolphin you need
an SD card: Config > Wii > Insert SD Card, put the `apps` folder on it, and
launch `boot.dol` from there. The JSON CONFIG screen on the main menu tells
you what was actually read.
