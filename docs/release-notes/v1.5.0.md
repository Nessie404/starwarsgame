### Lap times, for everyone

Every driver on the grid now keeps a stopwatch — you and all eleven AI.
The HUD shows the lap you are on, the one you just did, and your best.

A lap only counts when it is actually driven across the line. The rollout
from the grid is not a lap (it used to time as one — the test caught a
3.55-second "lap"), and neither is a checkpoint recovery, so falling off
a cliff can't hand you a personal best.

### The lap popup

Crossing the line puts your time in **your own viewport** — not a banner
across a split screen belonging to somebody else — and calls it out with a
different chime when it is a personal best.

### A live leaderboard

A narrow column down the right edge of each viewport: position, driver,
and the gap to the leader in seconds. It stays out of the road. On a split
screen it shows the sharp end plus your own row, so it stays legible in a
quarter of the picture.

### The rivals have names

The eleven AI drivers are now HOLT, RENARD, BASTIEN, OSEI, TANAHA,
DELGADO, CROSS, IBARRA, NORDLI, SOLANO and PETRAN, fixed to their grid
slots so the same rival is the same rival between races. They replace the
strategy label on the HUD, in the leaderboard, and in the results — the
strategy is still what they drive like, it just isn't their name any more.

Character, visual identity and per-driver strengths are still on the list;
this is the names.

### Choose your lap count before the race

A **LAPS** row in the pre-race menu: `AUTO` — which shows the count that
circuit would pick for itself — or 1 to 9. It goes through the same
validation as the JSON override, so nothing you can select can produce a
nonsense race.

### A digital tachometer

Revs as a number beside the bar, and the bar itself now reads as four
states rather than a gradient: grey while the engine is bogging below its
torque band, green through the useful range, amber in the shift window,
red at the limiter — with a mark showing where the shift window starts.
The rev range is yours to set in the new `instruments` block of
`settings.json` (`tacho_idle_rpm`, `tacho_redline_rpm`).
