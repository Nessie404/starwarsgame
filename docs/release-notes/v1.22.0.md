# WiiKart 1.22.0

An engineering release: the difficulty, team and career data shapes
added back in v1.19.0 and v1.20.0 now actually do something when a race
starts. There is still no menu to reach any of it, so this changes
nothing you'll notice on its own — it's the release that makes the
eventual garage menu cheap to build.

## What's wired up

- **Difficulty.** A preset now sets the lap count, scales the AI's
  aggression and skill together, leans AI car assignment toward cars
  matched to (or weaker than) your own, and can force a circuit's
  guardrails on or off.
- **Team mode.** Every human and AI car on a team flies that team's
  colour, and a combined per-team score adds up from how each car on it
  finishes.
- **Career mode.** A human who finished 3rd last race starts 3rd on the
  grid next time, instead of always at the back.

## What isn't wired up yet

None of this is reachable in the shipped game: nothing sets a
difficulty, joins a team, or carries a career result from one race to
the next, because there's no garage/setup menu screen with controls
for any of it yet. There's also no per-team HUD element, and no
save/load so a career actually survives closing the game. See
`TODO.md` for the rest of the list.

## Playing it

Download `wiikart.zip`, unzip, and open `wiikart.dol` in
[Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org) (File > Open). On a real Wii, copy the
`apps` folder to an SD card and launch it from the Homebrew Channel.

To edit `cars.json`, `settings.json` or `controls.json` in Dolphin you need
an SD card: Config > Wii > Insert SD Card, put the `apps` folder on it, and
launch `boot.dol` from there. The JSON CONFIG screen on the main menu tells
you what was actually read.
