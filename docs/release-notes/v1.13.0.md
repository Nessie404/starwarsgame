# WiiKart 1.13.0

One change, and it's a real one: the AI drives a racing line now, instead
of just hugging a fixed offset all lap.

## Pace comes from skill, not from which sheet a driver runs

Each AI's strategy sheet has always had a `line_bias`. Until now that was
a constant lateral offset held for the entire lap — the INSIDE sheet sat
0.55 of the way to one edge of the road, the CRUISER sheet sat wide on the
other. On a track whose corners happen to bend mostly one way (most of the
mountain passes here do, being modeled on real one-way roads), a fixed
offset like that quietly shortens the total distance driven regardless of
who is actually behind the wheel — so INSIDE was fast and CRUISER was slow
whatever driver they were given, and lap times tracked the sheet a lot
more closely than they tracked skill.

The fix is a real racing line. `Track` now carries `curv_signed`, a
per-sample field that keeps not just how sharp a bend is but which way it
turns. `ai_tactical_line` looks 14 meters up the road, reads the curvature
there, and leans the car toward that actual apex — how hard is now what
`line_bias` means (0 stays close to the centerline, 1 clips it hard),
rather than which side of the road to sit on all lap. A straight reads
almost no curvature, so the lean relaxes back to the centerline between
corners on its own, and the lean flips automatically from a left-hander to
a right-hander because the sign comes from the road, not from the driver.

Measured on Berthoud: give INSIDE and CRUISER — the two ends of the
commitment range — identical skill and identical learned corner
confidence, and their best laps land within 1.4% of each other. Skill on
its own is still worth 7.3% (0.86 vs 1.06). The defend-and-attack tactics
that shadow a chasing human or line up a run at the car ahead are
unchanged — they still layer on top of whatever the apex line is doing.

## Playing it

Download `wiikart.zip`, unzip, and open `wiikart.dol` in
[Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org) (File > Open). On a real Wii, copy the
`apps` folder to an SD card and launch it from the Homebrew Channel.

To edit `cars.json`, `settings.json` or `controls.json` in Dolphin you need
an SD card: Config > Wii > Insert SD Card, put the `apps` folder on it, and
launch `boot.dol` from there. The JSON CONFIG screen on the main menu tells
you what was actually read.
