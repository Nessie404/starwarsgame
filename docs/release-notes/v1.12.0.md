# WiiKart 1.12.0

Two requests from the TODO list, both now done: a marshal for driving the
wrong way, and a quieter HUD.

## A marshal helicopter for driving the wrong way

Point the car back down the mountain and drive, and a helicopter drops in
over your own viewport with a **TURN AROUND** message, the speed readout
turns red, and the engine is cut to 35% of normal until you turn around and
drive it back off.

It is judged on net progress along the track's centreline — the same
signed-arc measurement the lap counter already keeps — not on which way the
car happens to be pointing, so spinning out at a hairpin and ending up
facing the wrong way for a moment does not trip it. It takes 2.5 seconds of
genuinely losing ground before the helicopter shows up, and it clears at
the same rate it built: turn around and drive forward for as long as you
were going backward, and it leaves.

It only ever applies to the human players. The AI's own reverse-out
recovery after a spin is untouched — the whole point is that a human
driving the wrong way on purpose (or by accident) gets a consequence, not
that any car facing backward for a moment does.

Both numbers — how long it takes, and how much engine is left — are in the
new `wrong_way` block of `settings.json` if you want the marshal to be
quicker to arrive, or less forgiving once it has.

## A quieter HUD

The raw-input debug panel — every key, pad and Wii Remote binding, live,
useful for checking a new control mapping — used to be on by default,
covering a good chunk of the screen. It now defaults off; the small
always-on leaderboard is what a race screen shows day to day. Turn
`show_input_overlay` on in `controls.json` when you actually need to check
a mapping, same as before.

The setup screen's control legend was two lines explaining how to move the
selection and how to confirm it. It is one line now.

## Playing it

Download `wiikart.zip`, unzip, and open `wiikart.dol` in
[Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org) (File > Open). On a real Wii, copy the
`apps` folder to an SD card and launch it from the Homebrew Channel.

To edit `cars.json`, `settings.json` or `controls.json` in Dolphin you need
an SD card: Config > Wii > Insert SD Card, put the `apps` folder on it, and
launch `boot.dol` from there. The JSON CONFIG screen on the main menu tells
you what was actually read.
