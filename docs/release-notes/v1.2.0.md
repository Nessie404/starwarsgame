**Download `wiikart.zip`**, unzip it, and open `wiikart.dol` in
[Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org) — or copy the `apps` folder to an SD
card and launch it from the Homebrew Channel on a real Wii.

### Gearboxes

Every car now has a real gearbox, 4 to 6 speeds, with its own per-gear top
speeds. The engine bogs below the bottom third of a gear's range, pulls
flat through the band, falls away past peak and cuts at the limiter, and
every shift costs 0.18 s of drive. Pick **AUTO** or **MANUAL** in the
garage. Each AI has its own shift points and reaction delay, so the field
sounds — and drives — different into every corner.

### Two new passes, and cliffs that bite

| Circuit | Length | Corners | Tightest | Width | Max grade | Guardrails | Laps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Classic Circuit | 555 m | 10 | 24 m | 12 m | 3% | yes | 4 |
| Berthoud Pass | 1421 m | 19 | 11 m | 11 m | 15% | yes | 2 |
| Loveland Pass | 1699 m | 11 | 12 m | 10.8 m | 12% | **no** | 2 |
| Kenosha Pass | 2172 m | 43 | 15 m | 12 m | 10% | yes | 2 |
| Monarch Pass | 1612 m | 28 | 11 m | 7.6 m | 17% | **no** | 2 |

Loveland lost its guardrails and got wider and longer. **Kenosha Pass** is
the long one — 43 corners, and wide enough to carry speed if you commit.
**Monarch Pass** is the hard one: no rails, 7.6 m of road in places and the
steepest grades in the game.

Where there are no rails you can fall. Go over the edge and you are put
back on the road at the last checkpoint you passed, keeping your lap, the
way Mario does it. Lap counts are now set per circuit so every race runs
about the same distance instead of three laps of wildly different tracks.

### Menus you can back out of

Up/down picks a row, left/right changes that row's value, confirm
activates and **back steps out**. Clicking no longer advances the whole
page. A race also refuses to start with more players than there are
controllers connected — it says `NEED n PADS FOUND n` and hands you back
to the menu instead of breaking. Unplug a pad mid-race and you return to
the menu rather than lose the car.

### Keyboard defaults

- **Player 1:** WASD to drive, `E` shift up, `Q` shift down, `X` power-up, `Space` handbrake.
- **Player 2:** IJKL to drive, `O` shift up, `U` shift down, `M` power-up, `P` handbrake.
- **Players 3 and 4:** Wii Remote, GameCube/Xbox-style pad, or a second keyboard mirroring 1 and 2.

`Enter` confirms, `Esc` backs out, `R` returns to the menu.

### Garage

Car, paint, **gearbox** and **tire compound** — medium, soft or hard, each
with its own grip and drag, so tires are a real trade rather than a label.
The HUD gained a gear number and a rev bar.
