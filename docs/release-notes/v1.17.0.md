# WiiKart 1.17.0

Six new cars, and Berthoud Pass 2.0 rebuilt so it no longer runs into
itself.

## Six new cars

The garage grows from five to eleven:

- **BUGGY** — a 620 kg dune buggy with the best dirt grip in the garage
  (0.85) and not much else; weak on tarmac, unbothered by gravel.
- **WAGON** — a 1550 kg practical all-rounder, unremarkable everywhere,
  which is its own kind of dependable.
- **FORMULA** — a 720 kg open-wheel track car with the most lateral grip
  (1.45 g) and the shortest brakes (28 m) in the roster, and the worst
  dirt grip (0.10) to go with them. Magnificent on pavement, miserable
  off it.
- **TRUCK** — a 2100 kg pickup: 48 m to stop, 0.70 g of grip, but 0.78
  dirt grip and the mass to shrug off a rough shoulder.
- **HERITAGE** — an 890 kg vintage roadster. 85 hp and drum brakes that
  need 45 m to work, but light enough to still be thrown around.
- **MUSCLE** — a 1620 kg, 420 hp brute with the chassis to match: great
  in a straight line, a handful everywhere else.

Every one of them is a plain entry in `cars.json` — real-world units,
validated against the same ranges as the original five, nothing
special-cased in code.

## Berthoud Pass 2.0, rebuilt

The v1.15.0 version of this track raced fine, but a closer look at its
actual shape found two real problems that nothing in the test suite had
caught: several pieces of the road passed within a meter or two of each
other in plan view — including the valley loop-back clipping both the
climb and the base of the descent — and several of its hairpin apexes
turned as sharp as 90° to 161° at a single point.

The track is rebuilt from a cleaner shape: the climb and the descent
each follow a gentle sideways wiggle, kept in their own lane with a gap
wide enough that they can never reach each other, and the summit and
valley turns are wide, gradual arcs rather than sharp corners. Every
bend on the new lap has a radius of at least 37 m — for comparison nothing
else on the whole roster of circuits gets anywhere close to that gentle
— and the nearest any two unrelated pieces of pavement come to each
other is 96 m. The lap is shorter for it (1411 m, down from 3263 m) and
climbs less (67 m instead of 86 m): the honest cost of easing every
switchback out into a real curve.

## Playing it

Download `wiikart.zip`, unzip, and open `wiikart.dol` in
[Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org) (File > Open). On a real Wii, copy the
`apps` folder to an SD card and launch it from the Homebrew Channel.

To edit `cars.json`, `settings.json` or `controls.json` in Dolphin you need
an SD card: Config > Wii > Insert SD Card, put the `apps` folder on it, and
launch `boot.dol` from there. The JSON CONFIG screen on the main menu tells
you what was actually read.
