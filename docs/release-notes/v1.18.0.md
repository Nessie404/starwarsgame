# WiiKart 1.18.0

Drivetrain, more grip that breaks away later, and a fix for a bug that had
been keeping every car added since v1.15.0 out of the garage for most
players without them realizing it.

## Drivetrain

Every car in `cars.json` now has a `"drivetrain"` block: `"fwd"`, `"rwd"`,
or `"awd"` with a `front_bias` (0–1) for how the drive splits between the
axles. A car with no block at all is RWD, so every pre-1.18.0 car keeps
its old behavior unchanged.

- **AWD** gets off the line faster than an otherwise-identical FWD or RWD
  car — splitting the acceleration traction demand across two driven
  axles leaves more of the grip circle free for putting power down
  without either axle individually breaking loose.
- **FWD** understeers a little more once you're back on the throttle
  mid-corner — the same tires are steering and driving.
- **RWD** gets correspondingly looser and easier to rotate under power
  instead.

RALLY, TOURER, BUGGY, and TRUCK are AWD (with bias values reflecting how
each is built — TOURER splits close to even, TRUCK leans rear); RUBY and
WAGON are front-wheel drive; the rest of the roster stays rear-wheel
drive. See `config/README.md` for the full block syntax.

## More grip, later breakaway

Every car's lateral grip is up about 25% across the board. The scrub that
kicks in once a corner is driven past that limit is gentler than before,
so running out of grip now reads as a progressive scrub that deepens the
further past the limit you push, rather than an abrupt wall right at the
edge.

## Fixed: cars going missing from the garage

`main.c` falls back to a small compiled-in car list whenever the game has
no SD card to read `cars.json` from — the common case when a release DOL
is opened directly in an emulator without setting up a virtual SD card.
That compiled list still only had the original four cars in it, so RUBY
(added in v1.15.0) and BUGGY/WAGON/FORMULA/TRUCK/HERITAGE/MUSCLE (added in
v1.17.0) were invisible in the garage for anyone running that way — even
though `cars.json` itself was correct and had been shipping in every
release zip all along. The compiled list now mirrors `cars.json` exactly,
eleven cars deep.

Tracking that fix down also turned up a second, related bug: RUBY's own
gear ratios had a first-to-second-gear jump more than twice as steep as
every other car's, which could strand the AI gearbox unable to ever shift
out of first gear. It never showed up before because RUBY had never
actually been assigned to an AI driver — now that every car is in play,
it is fixed too.

## Playing it

Download `wiikart.zip`, unzip, and open `wiikart.dol` in
[Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org) (File > Open). On a real Wii, copy the
`apps` folder to an SD card and launch it from the Homebrew Channel.

To edit `cars.json`, `settings.json` or `controls.json` in Dolphin you need
an SD card: Config > Wii > Insert SD Card, put the `apps` folder on it, and
launch `boot.dol` from there. The JSON CONFIG screen on the main menu tells
you what was actually read.
