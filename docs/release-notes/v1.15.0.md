# WiiKart 1.15.0

Boost moves off the track and onto the engine, a real Berthoud Pass joins
the roster, and Ruby shows up with a high-pressure turbo.

## Boost is an engine trait now

The old push-to-pass power-up is gone. In its place, cars have an
**aspiration** — naturally aspirated, turbocharged, or supercharged — set
in `cars.json` and managed entirely by the driver, not collected on the
course.

A **turbo** still works the way it did in 1.14: hold the button and the
engine gets real extra power for as long as the charge lasts, and the
charge only recharges while you're off the throttle. New in 1.15, it also
spools — press the button and the boost ramps in over a fraction of a
second rather than snapping to full power, the way a real turbo actually
builds boost pressure.

A **supercharger** is simpler and has no button at all: it's a flat power
multiplier that's just always there any time you're on the throttle, no
charge to manage, no lag to wait out — a belt-driven blower doesn't spool
or run out. `BLOWER` is the new car built to show it off.

```json
"aspiration": {
  "type": "turbo",
  "power_multiplier": 1.40,
  "boost_seconds": 2.5,
  "recharge_seconds": 5.0,
  "spool_seconds": 0.4
}
```

```json
"aspiration": {
  "type": "supercharged",
  "power_multiplier": 1.20
}
```

Fresh tires remain on the track as the one power-up left. The AI decide
for themselves when to spend a turbo's charge — on a straight, with real
charge banked, and only while they'd be accelerating anyway.

## Berthoud Pass 2.0

A new eighth circuit, built from the real Berthoud Pass's own elevation
profile: three switchbacks climbing one side, a short summit, four more
switchbacks descending the other, a flat loop through the valley floor
that turns the road around, and a gentle climb back up to a start/finish
that sits at the lap's own middle elevation — below the summit, above the
valley floor it drops to. 3263 m, 86 m of climb, 33 corners.

## RUBY

A new lightweight, high-horsepower, turbocharged car for the garage: six
gears topping out at 15/35/67/100/150/200 km/h, a 4-cylinder engine that
punches well above its 1080 kg curb weight, and a high-pressure turbo
tune.

## Playing it

Download `wiikart.zip`, unzip, and open `wiikart.dol` in
[Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org) (File > Open). On a real Wii, copy the
`apps` folder to an SD card and launch it from the Homebrew Channel.

To edit `cars.json`, `settings.json` or `controls.json` in Dolphin you need
an SD card: Config > Wii > Insert SD Card, put the `apps` folder on it, and
launch `boot.dol` from there. The JSON CONFIG screen on the main menu tells
you what was actually read.
